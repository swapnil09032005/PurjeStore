# PurjeStore — Milestone 3.2 Data Anomaly & Structural Quality Detection
## Consolidated Validation and Disposition Report

**Status:** COMPLETE  
**Scope:** 177 read-only CSV working sources under `data/raw`.

## 1. Executive summary

Milestone 3.2 established and executed an evidence-first framework for detecting potential data-quality and structural anomalies. The process covered:

- assessment framework;
- business/identifier uniqueness;
- referential and cross-table consistency;
- numeric range/scale/magnitude assessment;
- datetime validity and temporal consistency;
- categorical, placeholder, whitespace, case, and text-format assessment;
- workflow/status semantics and state-transition consistency.

The investigation followed the rule: **detect before correcting**. Raw CSVs were not modified, and unusual observations were not automatically classified as errors.

## 4. 3.2.2 — Business / identifier uniqueness

Identifier-like fields were investigated using semantic evidence. Repetition was not treated as duplication automatically. One-to-many references and repeating foreign/grouping identifiers were distinguished from true business-identifier duplication.

This distinction was essential for the later workflow-history investigation.

## 10. Cross-category methodological conclusion

The completed investigation establishes that an unusual observation is not automatically an anomaly.

Examples include:

- statistical outliers;
- repeated identifiers;
- missing values;
- placeholders;
- unparseable datetime candidates;
- multiple workflow states;
- timestamp ties;
- unusual status sequences.

The adopted evidence chain is:

```text
Structural signal
      ↓
Candidate anomaly
      ↓
Semantic investigation
      ↓
Cross-table / business-rule evidence
      ↓
Disposition
      ↓
Downstream treatment decision
```

## 11. Limitations retained

- Three workflow fields remain classified as false-history grouping cases.
- Eight same-timestamp state changes do not have a defensible within-timestamp ordering.
- Thirteen timestamp ties are retained as observations, not automatically anomalies.
- Numeric outliers are not automatically errors.
- Missing values are not automatically imputed or deleted.
- Raw data has not been corrected as part of anomaly detection.
