## 6. Approved Treatment Vocabulary

The project approved the following treatment categories:

  -----------------------------------------------------------------------
  Treatment                           Definition
  ----------------------------------- -----------------------------------
  `KEEP`                              Preserve the field/value
                                      representation because evidence
                                      supports retention.

  `NORMALIZE`                         Standardize representation without
                                      changing underlying business
                                      meaning.

  `CONVERT`                           Convert to an appropriate technical
                                      representation after semantic
                                      meaning is established.

  `MASK`                              Protect sensitive information while
                                      retaining an analytically useful
                                      representation where justified.

  `EXCLUDE`                           Exclude a field/value from a
                                      particular analytical dataset when
                                      appropriate for that analytical
                                      purpose.

  `QUARANTINE`                        Isolate a value/record from normal
                                      analytical processing pending
                                      investigation.

  `IMPUTE`                            Replace missing values using a
                                      justified, evidence-supported
                                      method.

  `LEAVE_UNRESOLVED`                  Do not transform because current
                                      evidence is insufficient to justify
                                      a treatment.
  -----------------------------------------------------------------------

These are decision categories, not automatic transformations.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 7. Treatment Decision Principles

The decision framework requires the following sequence:

1.  Identify exactly what was detected.
2.  Determine whether it is actually erroneous or merely unusual.
3.  Establish the field's semantic/business meaning.
4.  Review cross-table and structural evidence.
5.  Determine whether a safe transformation is possible.
6.  Determine whether the proposed transformation can be validated.
7.  Assign the least destructive justified treatment.
8.  Preserve unresolved cases when evidence is insufficient.

The framework intentionally favors preservation over unsupported
modification.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Part IV --- Treatment Interpretation

## 18. Meaning of `KEEP`

`KEEP` means that current evidence supports retaining the field/value
representation.

It does **not** mean:

-   the field must be used in every analytical dataset;
-   the field is automatically useful for machine learning;
-   the field is free of all possible quality issues;
-   no downstream validation is required.

Analytical suitability remains dependent on dataset grain and purpose.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 19. Meaning of `CONVERT`

`CONVERT` means that the field has a sufficiently established semantic
meaning to justify changing its technical representation during
controlled cleaning.

Conversion must still be implemented separately and validated with
before/after checks.

Examples identified in 3.3.2 include datetime conversion and other
semantically established technical representations.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 20. Meaning of `LEAVE_UNRESOLVED`

`LEAVE_UNRESOLVED` is an evidence status, not a statement that the field
is definitely defective.

It means:

> Current evidence is insufficient to justify a transformation safely.

This distinction is essential for recovered relational data.

An unresolved field may later be resolved using:

-   additional cross-table evidence;
-   business-rule evidence;
-   downstream analytical requirements;
-   domain-owner clarification;
-   row/value-level investigation;
-   or controlled analytical-dataset design.

No unsupported transformation should be introduced merely to eliminate
the unresolved count.

# Part VII --- Limitations and Boundaries

## 25. Current Limitations

### 25.1 Field-level scope

3.3.2 is primarily a field-level treatment specification.

Some future decisions may require:

-   row-level analysis;
-   value-level analysis;
-   record-state analysis;
-   entity-level analysis;
-   analytical-dataset-specific decisions;
-   business-process interpretation.

These should be performed when the relevant downstream use case is
defined.

### 25.2 `LEAVE_UNRESOLVED`

The remaining **221 fields** are not automatically errors.

They simply lack sufficient current evidence for a justified
transformation.

### 25.3 Analytical purpose

A field may be retained in a master cleaned layer but excluded from a
particular analytical dataset.

Therefore:

``` text
KEEP ≠ use everywhere
```

### 25.4 Missingness

Missing values should not be imputed merely because they exist.

Imputation requires:

-   semantic meaning;
-   analytical justification;
-   defensible assumptions;
-   appropriate statistical/business logic;
-   validation of the resulting distribution and relationships.

### 25.5 Workflow grain

Workflow analysis must continue to use validated business identity.

Incorrect grouping by a repeated foreign key can manufacture false
histories.
