# 5.3-C — Analytical Dataset Construction and Persistence Closure

## 1. Purpose

Major Milestone 5.3-C established and validated the controlled construction and physical persistence of the analytical datasets approved through the preceding analytical contract workflow.

The objective was not to construct generic e-commerce datasets or to perform uncontrolled joins. The objective was to take the 26 finalized analytical contracts from the preceding contract-finalization stages, construct them at their authorized source grain, validate the resulting analytical artifacts, and persist only those artifacts that passed the defined construction and persistence gates.

The work was performed using the actual PurjeStore cleaned data and previously validated analytical evidence.

No unsupported business interpretation was introduced during this milestone.

## 7. Grain-Key Reconciliation

An important validation finding arose during construction.

The 23 explicit grain keys identified during analytical contract specification were validated as source-grain validation keys. They were not necessarily requested analytical output fields.

The initial construction attempt exposed this distinction because requiring every source-grain validation key to appear in the analytical output would have incorrectly altered the requested analytical schemas.

A dedicated reconciliation audit therefore established:

* 23 explicit grain keys available in the cleaned sources;
* 23 explicit grain keys populated;
* 23 explicit grain keys duplicate-free;
* 23 explicit grain keys unique among non-null values;
* 23 explicit grain keys not injected into analytical output schemas.

This distinction was preserved in the final construction.

The construction process did not manufacture or inject grain fields merely to satisfy a validation rule.
