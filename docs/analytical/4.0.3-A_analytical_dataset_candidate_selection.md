## 5. Candidate Selection Methodology

The 52 candidates validated in 4.0.2-C were used as the authoritative candidate universe. The workflow does not regenerate candidates from generic identifier-like columns.

For each candidate, the workflow validates source availability, grain field availability, relationship participation, fan-out disposition, pre-aggregation disposition, and analytical-anchor disposition.

Where the authoritative relationship file represents endpoints through qualified source_column and target_column values rather than separate source_table and target_table columns, table identities are recovered only when the authoritative endpoint representation supports that parsing.

Unqualified endpoints are not assigned guessed table identities.

## 14. Methodological Decisions

- The 4.0.2-C 52-candidate register is authoritative for candidate selection.
- Generic *_id discovery is not used to invent candidates.
- Relationship endpoints are interpreted from their actual authoritative representation.
- Unqualified relationship endpoints are not assigned guessed table identities.
- One-to-many relationships are not automatically treated as errors.
- Uncontrolled analytical fan-out is the relevant construction risk.
- Pre-aggregation remains required where the intended analytical grain requires it.
- Candidate selection is not equivalent to business-problem selection.
- Candidate selection is not equivalent to final analytical-dataset approval.

## 15. Limitations

- Final analytical dataset grain and source scope have not yet been locked.
- No production analytical dataset has yet been constructed in this task.
- Structural candidate suitability does not establish a validated business question.
- Any unresolved grain issue must be explicitly dispositioned before construction.
- Future joins require empirical validation at the intended analytical grain.
- Business KPIs and ML targets remain evidence-dependent and are intentionally not forced.
