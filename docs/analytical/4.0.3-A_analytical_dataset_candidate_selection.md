## 7. Candidate Selection Methodology

The 52 candidates are inherited from the validated 4.0.2-C architecture review. No generic identifier-column heuristic was used to create new candidates.

- Candidate table identity
- Cleaned source availability
- Candidate grain-field representation
- Actual cleaned-schema grain-field existence
- Validated relationship participation
- Fan-out disposition
- Pre-aggregation disposition
- Analytical-anchor disposition

A missing grain field is retained as a review condition rather than being replaced by an invented key.

## 14. Methodological Decisions

- The 4.0.2-C candidate register is the authoritative candidate universe.
- Generic *_id heuristics are not used to invent analytical candidates.
- The authoritative relationship file is interpreted according to its actual source_column/target_column schema.
- Concrete relationship endpoints are derived from table.field representations.
- Semantic or separately represented relationships are not converted into invented concrete joins.
- One-to-many relationships are not automatically treated as analytical errors.
- Uncontrolled fan-out is the key join-risk concern.
- Aggregation-before-joining is required where the intended grain requires it.
- Candidate selection is not final business-problem selection.

## 15. Limitations

- 4.0.3-A does not lock the final source scope of a business analytical dataset.
- 4.0.3-A does not construct production analytical datasets.
- Structural candidate suitability does not prove business usefulness.
- Any unresolved grain issue must be dispositioned before construction.
- Future join paths must be validated at the intended analytical grain.
- Business KPIs and ML targets remain evidence-dependent and unselected.

## 16. Reproducibility
