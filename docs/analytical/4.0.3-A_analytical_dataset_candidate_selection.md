## 5. Authoritative Relationship Endpoint Schema

The authoritative relationship file contains 69 rows and eight columns. It does not contain separate `source_table` or `target_table` columns.

The authoritative columns are:

- `source_column`
- `target_column`
- `relationship_type`
- `validation_status`
- `source_total_or_tested`
- `populated_or_validated`
- `unmatched_count`
- `evidence_or_rule`

Concrete relationship endpoints are encoded as qualified strings in `source_column` and `target_column`, for example:

```text
orders.userId
user.user_id
```

The endpoint parser derives the table and field components only for concrete qualified endpoints. It does not invent a concrete target for the dynamic relationship.

## 14. Methodological Decisions

- The 4.0.2-C reconciled candidate register is the authoritative candidate universe.
- Generic identifier heuristics are not used to create new candidates.
- Relationship table and field endpoints are derived only from the authoritative qualified endpoint representation.
- The dynamic target relationship is preserved without inventing a concrete target.
- One-to-many structure is not automatically treated as an analytical error.
- Uncontrolled fan-out is the relevant construction risk.
- Pre-aggregation remains mandatory where required by analytical grain.
- Candidate structural suitability does not equal business-question validation.
- No final KPI, ML target, or business-impact claim is made at this stage.

## 15. Limitations

- 4.0.3-A does not yet lock final source scope for business analytical datasets.
- 4.0.3-A does not construct production analytical datasets.
- Candidate selection does not establish a validated business problem.
- Future joins must be validated at the intended analytical grain.
- Any unresolved grain issue must be dispositioned before construction.
