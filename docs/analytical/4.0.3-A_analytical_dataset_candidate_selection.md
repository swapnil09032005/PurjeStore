## 15. Important Methodological Decisions

- The 4.0.2-C candidate register is the authoritative candidate universe.
- The relationship file is interpreted according to its actual schema.
- Composite `table.field` endpoint values are parsed directly.
- Relationship classification is based on endpoint representation, not broad keyword matching.
- No generic identifier heuristic is used to invent candidates.
- One-to-many relationships are not automatically considered errors.
- Fan-out risk is controlled at the intended analytical grain.
- Aggregation-before-joining remains required where relationship structure demands it.
- Semantic or separately represented relationship evidence is not assigned an invented concrete target.
- Candidate selection does not constitute final business-problem or KPI approval.

## 16. Limitations

- 4.0.3-A does not lock final analytical source scope.
- 4.0.3-A does not construct production analytical datasets.
- Structural candidate suitability does not prove business usefulness.
- Any unresolved grain issue must be resolved or explicitly dispositioned before construction.
- Future joins must be validated at the intended analytical grain.
- Business KPIs and ML targets remain intentionally unselected until supported by analytical evidence.
