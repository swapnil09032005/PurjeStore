## 14. Methodological Decisions

- The 4.0.2-C register is the authoritative candidate universe.
- Generic identifier heuristics are not used to invent candidates.
- Actual cleaned CSV headers are used for grain-field checks.
- The actual 8-column relationship artifact schema is respected.
- Nonexistent `source_table` and `target_table` columns are not assumed.
- Concrete relationships are determined from actual endpoint representation.
- Semantic/polymorphic evidence is not converted into invented concrete endpoints.
- One-to-many relationships are not automatically rejected.
- Fan-out prevention remains a construction-stage control.
- Candidate selection does not equal business-problem selection.

## 15. Limitations

- 4.0.3-A does not construct production analytical datasets.
- 4.0.3-A does not approve final business KPIs.
- 4.0.3-A does not select ML targets.
- Some grain fields may require further review before construction.
- The authoritative relationship CSV does not expose source/target tables as separate columns.
- Later construction must validate joins at the intended analytical grain.
