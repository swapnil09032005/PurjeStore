## 9. KPI activation gate

A candidate metric may be activated as a final business KPI only after a separate evidence-backed approval establishes all of the following:
- Business meaning and intended decision use.
- Exact source dataset and source field(s).
- Documented grain and population.
- Aggregation formula, units, null handling, and exclusions.
- Temporal definition and valid date semantics, if applicable.
- Relationship and join requirements, including cardinality and fan-out controls, if more than one dataset is needed.
- Reconciliation against the analytical source.
- Interpretation limits and explicit approval recorded in project evidence.

Until that gate is satisfied, the metric remains a candidate and must not be labelled as an approved KPI.
