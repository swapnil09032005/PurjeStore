## 1. Authoritative Definition

Repository evidence defines 4.0.3-C as the controlled step after 4.0.3-B that validates relationship join paths and fan-out behavior before any analytical dataset construction begins.

The definition is derived from the completed 4.0.3-B documentation, which explicitly names the next milestone as `4.0.3-C - Validate join paths and fan-out controls`, and from the 4.0.1 analytical dataset framework requiring cardinality and fan-out validation before construction.

## 2. Objective

The objective of 4.0.3-C is to profile validated relationship paths against the cleaned PurjeStore data and determine which paths are safe, risky, partial, blocked, or deferred for downstream analytical construction decisions.

4.0.3-C does not construct analytical datasets and does not approve business KPIs, ML targets, or business-impact claims.

## 6. Relationship Expansion Rules

- Direct endpoints such as `orders.order_id` remain one path endpoint.
- Multi-target endpoint strings such as `vendors.vendor_id / vendors.user_id` are expanded into separate alternatives.

- The polymorphic `ops_audit_log.entity_id -> target by entity_type` relationship is preserved but not converted into a fabricated concrete join.

## 10. Invoices Relationship-Count Investigation

4.0.3-B carried forward an `invoices` relationship-count issue: the repaired 4.0.2-C register recorded 9 relationships, while 4.0.3-B recomputation found 8 direct concrete table-participation records.

4.0.3-C recomputed direct relationship records involving the `invoices` table from `VALIDATED_RELATIONSHIPS.csv`: **8**.

The direct record numbers are:

`47, 48, 49, 50, 51, 52, 55, 61`

A broader text search for invoice-related relationship evidence found **10** records because it includes related e-invoice / e-way-bill evidence that does not always directly participate through the `invoices` table.

Conclusion: the mismatch is a relationship-qualification difference between a carried candidate-level count and direct concrete table participation from the authoritative relationship file. It is documented rather than overwritten. Downstream construction must use path-level evidence from the 4.0.3-C register instead of relying on the older aggregate count alone.

## 13. Decisions

- 4.0.3-C validates relationship paths, not final analytical datasets.
- Fan-out risk is measured directionally from source endpoint to target endpoint.
- Dynamic polymorphic evidence is preserved without inventing a target table.
- Multi-target and wildcard relationship evidence is expanded into auditable path alternatives.
- Older aggregate relationship counts should not override path-level evidence.

## 14. Limitations and Deferred Work

- 4.0.3-C does not choose final business analytical datasets.
- 4.0.3-C does not aggregate child tables or build production analytical outputs.
- Paths with fan-out risk require pre-aggregation or exclusion decisions in the construction-design stage.
- Paths with partial/no referential matches require explicit inclusion/exclusion decisions before construction.
