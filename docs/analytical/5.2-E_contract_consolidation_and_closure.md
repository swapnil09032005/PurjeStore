## 2. Purpose

Milestone 5.2-E consolidates the validated evidence produced by the preceding analytical-contract stages and determines whether the candidates that passed the 5.2-C evidence gate have corresponding analytical contract specifications from 5.2-D.

The purpose of this stage is contract consolidation and closure.

This stage does **not** construct analytical datasets, perform business-data joins, finalize KPIs, select machine-learning targets, or make causal or business-impact claims.

The consolidation therefore acts as a controlled handoff from evidence-based candidate qualification into later analytical work.

## 15. Handoff to Subsequent Analytical Work

The completed contracts establish an evidence-controlled foundation for subsequent analytical work.

However, completion of an analytical contract does not by itself constitute:

a finalized business KPI;
a finalized business question;
a constructed analytical dataset;
a validated business-impact estimate;
a machine-learning target;
a causal relationship;
or a production dashboard metric.

Those decisions must be made in later stages using the validated contract specifications and the actual data evidence.

The four deferred candidates remain outside the finalized 5.2-D contract set until their unresolved evidence requirements are addressed.
