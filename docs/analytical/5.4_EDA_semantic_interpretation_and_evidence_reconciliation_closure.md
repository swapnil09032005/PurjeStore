## 9. Semantic Interpretation Boundary

The 5.4 semantic reconciliation establishes the following interpretation boundary for subsequent analysis:

> **EDA evidence is evidence about the observed data and its statistical/structural properties. It is not, by itself, a business conclusion.**

Consequently:

- observed variation does not automatically establish business importance;
- numerical magnitude does not automatically establish measure semantics;
- missingness does not automatically establish business failure;
- concentration does not automatically establish a business problem;
- temporal variation does not automatically establish a causal trend;
- repeated values do not automatically establish redundancy;
- source availability does not automatically establish analytical suitability;
- a populated field does not automatically establish semantic validity.

These distinctions must remain active during subsequent substantive EDA.
