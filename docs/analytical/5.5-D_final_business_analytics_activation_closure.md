# Major Milestone 5.5-D — Final Business-Analytics Activation Closure

## 1. Purpose

Major Milestone 5.5-D establishes the final evidence-backed activation boundary for the analytical candidates identified through the preceding Milestone 5 stages.

The objective was to translate validated 5.5-C analytical interpretation into controlled analytical activation while preserving semantic, grain, identifier, population, and evidence limitations.

This milestone does **not** constitute final KPI selection. It establishes which analytical candidates and finalized analytical contracts are eligible for subsequent business-analytics development subject to their documented limitations.

The activation process was deliberately constrained to the evidence supported by the PurjeStore data and the preceding validated analytical contracts.

## 4. Candidate-Measure Activation

The authoritative 193-row candidate-measure population was preserved without alteration.

The resulting activation register preserved all 193 candidate-measure rows.

Activation was controlled according to the evidence and limitations inherited from earlier milestones.

Candidate states included:

* evidence-backed descriptive or trend review eligibility;
* conditional analytical review where semantic limitations exist;
* identifier/code semantic controls;
* grain-limited analytical review;
* semantic deferral where interpretation remains unresolved.

The activation register is therefore an eligibility and evidence-control layer rather than a final KPI register.

## 5. Semantic Deferrals

Four semantic deferrals were preserved from 5.5-C:

1. `variants_temp → deleted`
2. `variants_temp → handling_time`
3. `variants_temp → item_length`
4. `variants_temp → stock`

These fields remain deferred because the available temporal evidence does not establish sufficiently reliable analytical semantics for activation.

### Important boundary: `item_length`

`variants_temp → item_length` is an authoritative semantic deferral but is not represented as a candidate-measure row in the 193-row candidate-measure register.

It was therefore preserved as a **semantic-only deferral**.

No artificial candidate-measure record was created for it.

This distinction prevents the analytical activation layer from manufacturing evidence that does not exist in the authoritative candidate population.

## 10. KPI Boundary

No final KPI was selected during 5.5-D.

No generic e-commerce KPI family was automatically introduced.

Examples of common e-commerce concepts such as revenue, orders, conversion, customer lifetime value, churn, retention, profitability, inventory performance, shipping performance, or marketing performance were not activated merely because they are common industry concepts.

Any future KPI must remain traceable to:

1. an actual PurjeStore field or validated analytical dataset;
2. an approved analytical question;

4. appropriate population scope;
5. appropriate temporal interpretation where applicable;
6. documented data-quality limitations;
7. semantic validation;
8. an explicit analytical definition.

## 14. Key Analytical Decision

The principal methodological decision in 5.5-D was to preserve the distinction between:

**analytical activation**,
**candidate-measure eligibility**, and
**final KPI selection**.

The milestone activates evidence-backed analytical opportunities without converting them into predetermined business KPIs.

The treatment of `variants_temp → item_length` demonstrates this boundary explicitly: the field remains a valid authoritative semantic deferral even though it is not part of the 193-row candidate-measure population.

No artificial analytical candidate was created to satisfy a validation structure.

## 15. Limitations Carried Forward

The following limitations remain relevant to subsequent business-analytics work:

1. Three datasets retain source-grain/business-grain limitations.
2. Four `variants_temp` temporal semantic fields remain deferred.
3. Three identifier/code warnings require semantic control.
4. `notification_logs` and `orders` remain analytical evidence-coverage gaps.
5. Candidate activation does not establish that every candidate should become a published KPI.
6. Descriptive evidence does not establish causality or business impact.
7. Numeric representation does not by itself establish business-measure semantics.
8. Any subsequent aggregation, joining, KPI definition, or business interpretation must remain consistent with the validated analytical contracts.
